# PSP Lifecounter

A Magic: the Gathering Lifecounter for your PSP!

## Controls:

Left - Decrease Player One Life Total
Right - Increase Player One Life Total

Circle - Decrease Player Two Life Total
Square - Increase Player Two Life Total

Start - Reset Life Totals to 20

## Installation

1. Download the latest release from [Releases](https://github.com/danielliegl/psp-lifecounter/releases).
2. Put the contents of the zip into `PSP/GAME` on your PSP's memory.
